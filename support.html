Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Questions

Where is Resolute after I open it?

Resolute lives in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen, as a small screen in front of a larger one. It has no Dock icon or window. If you don't see it, your menu bar may be full: quit another menu bar app, or on a MacBook with a notch, try with fewer icons.

I chose a resolution and my display went blank. What now?

Wait 15 seconds. After every change Resolute asks whether to keep it, and without an answer it switches back to the previous resolution on its own. You can also press Esc to revert right away.

What do the ⚡ resolutions mean?

They are HiDPI modes: the desktop is drawn at twice the pixels and scaled to your display, so text and images look sharper. Standard modes are drawn at the display's pixels as they are.

Why aren't all resolutions listed?

Like macOS, Resolute hides low-resolution copies of HiDPI sizes and very small modes. Choose "Show All Resolutions" at the bottom of a resolution menu, or turn it on in Settings, to see everything your display offers.

How do the keyboard shortcuts work?

Put the pointer on a display and press ⌃⌥⌘↑ for the next larger resolution or ⌃⌥⌘↓ for the next smaller one. You can turn the shortcuts off in Settings.

Does dimming change my monitor's backlight?

No. Resolute dims the picture it sends to an external display, which also lets it go darker than the monitor's own lowest setting. The monitor's backlight stays as you set it with its buttons.

How do I start Resolute when I log in?

Open Resolute's menu, then Settings, and turn on "Launch at Login".

How do I uninstall Resolute?

Choose "Quit Resolute" from its menu, then move Resolute from the Applications folder to the Trash.
