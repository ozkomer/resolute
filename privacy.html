Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Resolute is a Mac app that changes the resolution, refresh rate and brightness of your displays. It is developed by Ömer Özkan.

Data we collect

None. Resolute does not collect, store or share any personal information or usage data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Network

Resolute does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings, such as the profiles you save and your preferences, are stored only on your Mac, in Resolute's own preferences, and never leave it. If you choose "Copy Display Report", Resolute copies information about your Mac and displays to the clipboard; it goes nowhere unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
