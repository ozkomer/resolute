Resolute

Every resolution your displays can do, including the sharp HiDPI modes macOS hides, one click away in the menu bar.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 12 or later

All the resolutions

Every mode of every display, with the HiDPI ones macOS's own settings keep hidden. The native resolution is marked; duplicates stay out of the way.

Safe switching

After every change Resolute asks whether to keep it, and switches back on its own after 15 seconds, so a mode your display can't show fixes itself.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌃⌥⌘↑ and ⌃⌥⌘↓ step the display under the pointer to the next larger or smaller resolution.

Profiles

Save the resolutions of all your displays under a name, and bring the whole layout back in one click.

Exact refresh rates

59.94 Hz shown as 59.94 Hz, and every rate a ProMotion display offers, one submenu away.

Private

No account, no network access, no data collected. Your settings stay on your Mac.
